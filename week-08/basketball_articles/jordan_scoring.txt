Winning multiple scoring titles is well-correlated with Hall of Fame performance as each player with three or more such seasons is either in the Hall or on that trajectory. Let’s dive into some of the best and most consistent scorers to ever step on the court.
Michael Jordan – 10 Scoring Titles
This is a record that is likely to stand for some time if not forever. Among active players, Kevin Durant (4) and James Harden (3) have accomplished this feat several times and are not even halfway to MJ’s total. Jordan won scoring title in his third NBA season and was not dethroned in any of the following six seasons. Showing the enduring greatness of Jordan’s scoring prowess, he also won the scoring title in his final season with the Bulls (1997-98). His most prolific season came in 1990 when Jordan averaged an absurd 33.6 points per game.
Wilt Chamberlain – 7 Scoring Titles
Wilt the Stilt came firing out of the gate when he won MVP, Rookie of the Year and the scoring title in his debut season. Chamberlain proceeded to become one of the most dominant scorers in league history and added scoring titles to his list of accolades in each of his first six seasons. His highest-scoring season is also the highest-scoring season in league history as Chamberlain averaged 50.4 points per game. He added one more scoring title in 1966.
Kevin Durant – 4 Scoring Titles
KD has the most NBA scoring titles among active players. The Durantula has been a scoring machine since his rookie season at age 19 and if not for injury issues later on into his career, it’s possible Durant would have more scoring titles at this stage. Durant won three consecutive scoring titles from 2009-10 to 2011-12 and added another in 2013-14 while with the Thunder. He is still scoring at an elite clip following his 19th season after putting up 26.0 points per game in the 2025-26 season.
George Gervin – 4 Scoring Titles
The Iceman himself is next on the list with four scoring titles during his legendary playing career. Gervin helped make the Spurs’ transition from the ABA to the NBA a smooth one as he won the scoring title in the San Antonio’s second NBA season. That marked the first of three consecutive scoring titles for Gervin, who then added another in the 1982 season. Gervin had several notable scoring outbursts including a 63-point game against the New Orleans Jazz in 1978.
